THE NASH EQUILIBRIUM

+

A situation where no player can improve their outcome by changing only their own strategy. Everyone’s best move depends on everyone else’s.

THE COOPERATION QUESTION

+

What is best for you may not be best for everyone. The prisoner’s dilemma explores why individually tempting choices can produce a worse collective outcome.

THINKING BACKWARDS

+
