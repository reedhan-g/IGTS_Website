# IGTS DTU — The Strategy Room

The Strategy Room is a website concept for the Indian Game Theory Society at Delhi Technological University. A red chess knight sits on the line between the dark and light halves of the opening poster and moves as you scroll.

## The Playroom

**Last Hex** is the featured game. Two players take turns filling a board of 21 hexagons. Under the classic rules, the player with the lower total around the last empty hex wins. [Play Last Hex](https://last-hex-igts.onrender.com/) · [View the game source](https://github.com/reedhan-g/Last_Hex_IGTS)

Chess, Prisoner’s Dilemma, and Nim appear as *Coming soon* cards.

## Around the society

- **On Campus** highlights Grand Nashify, CodeLocks, and Neural Nash. [IGTS DTU on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/igts.dtu/)
- **Behind the Moves** explores Nash equilibrium, cooperation in the prisoner’s dilemma, and backward induction.
